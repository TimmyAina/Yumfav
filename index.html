Recipes & Prep Hacks

How To Keep It Fresh & Juicy

We don't just cook your food — we teach you the prep hacks to make healthy eating effortless.

💧

The 3-Minute Moisture Hack

Never eat dry chicken again. When microwaving your Yumfav bowl, drape a damp paper towel loosely over the tray. The trapped steam rehydrates the grains and keeps chicken breast tender and juicy.

❄️

Fridge vs. Freezer Rules

Keep 3 days of meals in the main chiller section. Store Thursday & Friday meals in the freezer upon delivery. Move them to the fridge the night before eating for effortless thawing without texture loss.

⚡

Anabolic Fuel Timing

Eat high-carb bowls (like Quinoa Jollof or Fried Rice) 90 minutes before heavy lifting sessions for glycogen saturation. Opt for Low-Carb Cauliflower Stir-fries on rest days for rapid fat burning.
