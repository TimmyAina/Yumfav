Recipes & Prep Hacks

How To Keep It Fresh & Juicy

We don't just cook your food — we teach you the prep hacks to make healthy eating effortless.

💧

The 3-Minute Moisture Hack

Never eat dry chicken again. When microwaving your Yumfav bowl, drape a damp paper towel loosely over the tray. The trapped steam rehydrates the grains and keeps chicken breast tender and juicy.

❄️

Fridge vs. Freezer Rules

Keep 3 days of meals in the main chiller section. Store Thursday & Friday meals in the freezer upon delivery. Move them to the fridge the night before eating for effortless thawing without texture loss.

⚡

Anabolic Fuel Timing

Eat high-carb bowls (like Quinoa Jollof or Sweet Potato Mash) 90 minutes before heavy lifting sessions for glycogen saturation. Opt for Low-Carb Cauliflower Stir-fries on rest days for rapid fat burning.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How do you calculate and verify the macros? ▼
Every protein cut, carb source, and healthy fat is weighed to the exact gram before cooking using laboratory-grade digital scales. We account for cooking yield and cold-pressed oil absorption so the nutritional stats on your label are accurate.
Which locations in Lagos do you deliver to? ▼
We deliver across Lagos Island (Lekki Phase 1, Ikoyi, Victoria Island, Oniru, Chevron, Ajah) and Lagos Mainland (Ikeja GRA, Maryland, Surulere, Yaba). Deliveries go out in temperature-controlled insulated bags.
Can I customize my meals or swap ingredients? ▼
Yes! When ordering via WhatsApp, let us know your allergies (e.g. no peanuts, no dairy, gluten sensitivity) or ingredient preferences (e.g. swap brown rice for extra veggies). We tailor your box to your needs.
How long do the meals stay fresh? ▼
Our vacuum-sealed meal prep trays stay fresh in your refrigerator chiller for up to 4 days, or up to 3 weeks if stored in the freezer. Our batch deliveries on Monday & Wednesday guarantee you always have peak freshness.
